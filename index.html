WHY THIS EXISTS

I kept hitting the same wall. A genuinely useful app, free at first, then a paywall shows up. Or it wants an account before I'm even allowed to try it. Or it's quietly selling what it learns about me.

So I started building the versions I actually wanted to use — free, no account unless the app genuinely needs one, nothing sold. Setoff was the first. It won't be the last.

— The person reinventing the wheel
